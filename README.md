# Balance: A wellness-focused planner for college students
Combines task planning, a focus timer, and a digital journal in one simple place.

## The problem
College students juggle assignments, exams, and personal responsibilities, and many already struggle with motivation and burnout. Most planning tools feel complicated or stressful, and wellness apps are usually separate from where students actually manage their work.

## Who it's for
College students who want to stay organized and make steady progress without feeling overwhelmed.

## User stories
1. As a college student who struggles with motivation, I want to break large assignments into smaller tasks and get reminders to take breaks, so I can avoid procrastinating and make steady progress.
2. As a college student, I want to track assignments, exams, and personal responsibilities in one place, with simple ways to prioritize and adjust when plans change, so I stay organized without complicated planning.
3. As a busy college student, I want a platform that encourages breaks, journaling, sleep, and self-care, so I can keep a healthier balance between school and personal life.

## Features
- **Plan:** break big assignments into smaller steps and prioritize them
- **Focus timer:** work sessions with built-in breaks
- **Journal:** a space to write and reflect, inspired by note-taking apps like GoodNotes
- Simple, calm interface with light and dark mode

## How I built it
I built this in UNC's Product Lab, a hands-on product management program. I followed the product development process: interviewed students about how they currently manage tasks and stress, defined the problem, wrote user stories, then prototyped and iterated.


## Tech
HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Entries are saved in the browser, so nothing leaves your device.
